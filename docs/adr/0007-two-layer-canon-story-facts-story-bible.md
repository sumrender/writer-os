# Two-layer canon: graded Story Facts store, synthesized Story Bible

Issue #12's architecture splits canon into two layers with different jobs (ADR-0007): the **Story Facts** store — atomic, per-chapter, source-verifiable facts versioned as of chapter N (append-only per ADR-0002) that every benchmark grade and consistency check operates on — and the **Story Bible** — the author-facing document (overview, profiles, summaries, timelines, rollups) synthesized from those facts plus the chapter prose via the `Synthesize` port, never graded as canon itself. We chose the split because a single synthesized document cannot serve both masters: synthesis output is generated prose that no assertion can verify exactly, so routing it into the fact layer would make grading and consistency checking judge-mediated everywhere, while routing raw facts into the author layer would give writers a queryable dump instead of a readable document. The `location` fact kind (issue #14) marks the boundary precisely: places enter canon as verifiable facts, and any richer location description is synthesized into the Bible from them.

Consequences: synthesis output crosses a trust boundary and is validated like extraction output (unknown fields dropped, missing fields rejected, ambiguous payloads hard-fail); the two layers stay synchronized by construction — every bible synthesis consumes the facts and summaries accumulated through the current ordinal — and per-ordinal bible snapshots mirror the facts snapshots so "the Bible as of chapter N" is queryable too.
